Your upstreams' registrations & who carries you

Your upstream accepting your routes is one step. Its transits then filter what it sends them, usually with a prefix list built from the as-set the upstream registered in PeeringDB. The first line shows whether that registration would include your prefix; the table shows which of its transits actually carry you right now (live RIS data) and why. One that reaches you another way, your own neighbour or a network behind another upstream of yours, isn't a gap: a network passes on only its best route.
