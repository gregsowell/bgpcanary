The tree starts at your AS and follows each RIS peer's AS path outward; the slider starts at "now", and while it sits there the tree refreshes every minute. {% if summ|length > 1 %}All prefixes are shown by default; untick some to focus on one or a few. With 2 to 6 prefixes ticked, each branch carries one strand per prefix in that prefix's color, and a dashed strand means that prefix is missing on a branch that carries the others (the node turns orange), the typical sign of a filter that knows one of your prefixes but not another. With more than 6 ticked, they are combined into a single strand.{% endif %} Colors compare each branch with the start of the window, or with the healthiest point if you choose that: green is a new branch, orange lost more than half its peers, red dashed dropped out. The second layer lists each upstream's main transits and peers from the global table; red dotted branches marked "not carrying you" reach your upstream's own routes but none of yours.
